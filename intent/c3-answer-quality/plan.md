Public development controls can be built after plan approval. Independent labeling begins once the owner designates two people. The proposed assignment uses a DevOps practitioner who did not develop or tune the candidate as the primary reviewer and answer grader. A second qualified person independently adjudicates. If prior exposure compromises a role, the owner reassigns the affected cases before unblinding. Both people work from offline packets.

## Independent reviewer · Prepare the key, then grade blind

1. **Before seeing candidate answers**, receive the rubric, synthetic source packet, known development-family inventory and blank case sheet. Disclose any work on the candidate or prior exposure to proposed holdout families. Move an exposed family to development and replace it before the freeze. Keep held-out questions and keys in the reviewer’s private offline workspace.
2. **Deliver a frozen case key.** For each case, record its family, primary challenge, entry point, benign-control or uncertainty-control status, expected disposition, required facts and caveats with severity, prohibited claims, and acceptable paraphrases. For each item, give the source version plus field or passage and a short rationale. Record the case and key digests. Verify at least one development family and one distinct holdout family for each challenge. The runner receives only the inputs and synthetic sources needed to exercise the frozen candidate.
3. **After collection**, receive shuffled cases with neutral IDs, visible answer text, the evidence actually available to that run, and the frozen key. The owner or run operator retains the mapping to candidate revision and prior scores. Split each answer into checkable claims, including assumptions embedded in recommendations. Mark a claim **supported** when the run’s evidence establishes it, **contradicted** when the evidence conflicts with it, and **unverifiable** when the evidence cannot decide it. Record the source or the specific evidence gap and explain the judgment. Mark each required item present, absent or materially incorrect. Record the observed disposition, attribution errors, false blocks and disputed items.
4. **Hand off the original grade sheet.** A row identifies the case and answer digest, claim text or required-item ID, judgment, severity, source reference, rationale and any dispute. Account for every scheduled case, including collection failures. Freeze the first votes before adjudication. The reviewer’s deliverables are the keyed coverage manifest, frozen case keys, and the completed first-pass grade sheets.

## Adjudicator · Resolve critical calls and close each case

1. Receive the frozen rubric and keys, blinded answers, run-specific sources, and a neutral list of items to assess. Independently assess all critical expected items, alleged critical errors and disputes before seeing the first grader’s vote or rationale. Inspect every scheduled case for a missed critical claim or omission. Flag an ambiguous key or broken evidence link explicitly.
2. Compare votes after recording the second judgment. For each disagreement, record both original votes, the exact source or missing support, the applicable rubric rule, the final judgment and the reason. Report initial disagreements over all items independently double-graded. List items that only needed case sign-off separately. A genuinely defective key requires a new version and regrading. Preserve the original case outcome in the run record.
3. Deliver one final disposition per scheduled case with its answer and packet digests, critical findings, resolved disputes and any outstanding evidence gaps. Sign off on uncontested cases as well. Missing answers or unresolved critical judgments remain **incomplete**. The owner receives a redacted summary, coverage counts and the final decision log. The offline record retains role attestations and both original votes.

**Worked development example.** The inventory catalog says that redeploying the previous image still requires a reverse migration for schema changes. If a review promises that image rollback restores the schema, the primary reviewer records a critical contradiction against `catalog:inventory-service.rollback`. If it only recommends reverting the image and omits a required migration caveat, record the omission. The adjudicator checks the same source and answer span and records which finding applies. ## Proof and risk controls

- **Collector integrity.** Exercise both entry points, including a valid empty-source case. Swap two sessions, duplicate a result, remove a trace, change an answer digest and supply an unknown source. Each defective join must be detected. Keep the full schedule as the coverage denominator, group retries under the original case, and retain each attempt.
- **Rubric controls.** Accept a grounded paraphrase. Reverse a dependency edge, substitute a different service, invent live health, guarantee rollback or remove a required caveat. Each mutation must produce the corresponding finding. Also distinguish clarification, justified abstention, security block and dependency unavailability on benign cases.
- **Metric arithmetic.** Verify the report against a hand-counted development packet. Report supported claims / checkable claims, entailed citations / asserted claim–source pairs, missing required attributions, missing critical items / required critical items, critical contradictions, correct dispositions / uncertainty cases, false security blocks / benign cases, and unwarranted abstentions on answerable cases. Report scorable answers / scheduled cases and p50/p95 visible latency with sample sizes and cache condition. Show pooled, per-challenge and per-entry-point counts. Explain every zero denominator.
- **Pilot decision.** A pass requires all seven held-out challenges covered, every scheduled answer matched and adjudicated, zero critical contradictions or omissions, zero false security blocks on benign controls, and the expected disposition on every uncertainty case. Known gate breaches are failures. Missing evidence is incomplete. If both occur, display both. Support, entailment and latency remain descriptive under the approved pilot.
- **Leakage and privacy.** Run browser collection on loopback and scoring offline with provider access disabled. Strip identity and transport handles before preparing grader packets. Keep sealed inputs, keys, raw votes and the candidate mapping outside Git and CI uploads. CI gets public controls, sanitized aggregates, version digests and review status. The owner verifies the human sign-offs against the private decision log. A matching digest alone cannot establish independent review. After exposure, retire a held-out family to regression use. Any future blind run uses fresh families.

**Ready for review.** Engineering delivers a repeatable development run and validated report format. The independent reviewer supplies the frozen labels and first votes. The adjudicator supplies the final case decisions. The owner reviews the pilot outcome separately from this implementation plan.
